How I work

A system cannot fix a business with no priorities. So the order matters.

  1. Goals first

    • We get clear on what you want and what matters most right now.
    • No goal, no build. That keeps the work focused.

  2. Then the system

    • I map how the work should move: who does what, where it lives, what happens next.
    • You see the plan before anything gets built.

  3. Then the marketing

    • Content, funnels, and automation, built to fit the system.
    • Every piece has a job, so nothing gets posted just to post.

  4. Written down

    • I write the SOPs so your team knows what to do.
    • No one has to stand over them or ask twice.

About Dei

Hi, I am Claire Dei. Teacher by degree, marketing operations by profession.

I grew up in Cebu, the eldest of three. I was the kid who ranked first, ran the group projects, and made the tracker sheet nobody asked for.

I studied to be a teacher and graduated magna cum laude in social studies education. I started freelancing in college to stay in school and help my family: tutoring, video editing, building presentations. It became the way I got to live on my own terms.

In October 2024 I took on my first international client as a general virtual assistant. General meant everything: graphics, video, GoHighLevel, and whatever else landed that day. Doing everything taught me how the pieces connect.

Then AI got good at executing. But it only works when someone has thought through the system it runs in. So I decided to be the one who thinks, not just the one who does.

A good system means people know what to do without someone standing over them.

What I believe

How I think
